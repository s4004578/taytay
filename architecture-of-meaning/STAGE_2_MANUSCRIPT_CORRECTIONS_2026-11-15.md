Date: 2026-11-15

## Editorial decision
The 36-page *The Recursive Structure of Divine Justice: A Cinematic Research Atlas* (12 Nov 2026) is retained intact as the original theological case study, not used as the scope of the entire project. The master project is an interdisciplinary atlas of reality, consciousness, knowledge, meaning-making and the unknown.

## Evidence that motivates the revision
- Original Atlas, pp. 1–6: five axes (Knowledge, Power, Freedom, Consequences, Justice); four floors (textual exegesis, philosophical critique, psychological resonance, metaphysical modeling). On p. 6, crossing floors is permitted; confusing their evidential status is not.
- Original Atlas, p. 3: 85-node / 409-link conceptual network described as an interpretive constellation, not a validated empirical theory.
- *Stage 1: Data Loading and Validation*, pp. 1–3: describes a 100-node / 409-edge reconstruction, and claims successful reconciliation. The report's narrative is not a machine-readable diff or verification log.
- *Stage 2 — Interdisciplinary Integration & Evidence Audit*, pp. 1–3: proposes broadening to physics, consciousness, information, culture, metaphysics, and labels some reconciliation/database work completed. These are **claims requiring artifact-level confirmation**.
- *Data and Provenance Audit*, pp. 1–3: claims checksums, a searchable 409-edge ledger, and complete structural checks. The PDF alone does not provide the hashes, the full ledger or a reproducible run. Mark those deliverables as **not yet independently confirmed**.
- *Collective “Spells” as Metaphor*, p. 1: analyzes social influence and language rather than offering evidence of supernatural control.

## Mandatory corrections to the master manuscript
1. Replace any framing that treats divine justice as the whole project with **one historical-textual case study** within a wider research program.
2. Replace '240 independently verified connections' with '240 proposed connections' unless an actual independent source-by-source verification log is provided.
3. Never equate 85-node canonical, 100-node reconstruction and 42-node prototype solely because some counts overlap. Preserve independent version identifiers, original IDs and source files.
4. Replace 'all 409 edges verified' with '409 proposed relationships; structural and evidentiary audits are distinct'. Structural validity requires a reproducible endpoint/ID/type check; source verification requires individually inspectable evidence.
5. Do not claim an SQLite database, SHA-256 archive or edge-by-edge spreadsheet exists merely because a report describes one. Require an accessible artifact and validation record.
6. Distinguish source-documented claims, empirical observations, formal theoretical claims, philosophical inferences, cultural interpretation, metaphor, and untested metaphysical speculation.
7. Do not use a quantum-mechanics term to imply evidence for cosmic or collective consciousness unless an actual mechanism and independent supporting research are supplied.
8. Preserve theological debate material as an unauthenticated secondary reconstruction until the user authorizes separate audio analysis.

Every revision should retain original wording and identify the revision as derived.
